Ethics

Medication and pigment autonomy

Ordinate distinguishes voluntary medical treatment from coercive social compliance, while acknowledging that medicine can be used by systems of control.

Public position

Pigment is not itself a disease. Treatment is justified when a patient experiences distress, injury, risk, loss of control, recovery burden or medically relevant instability.

Suppression therapies require consent, legal authority where applicable, medical review, safety reporting and a documented path back to autonomy.

Policy boundary

No Ordinate civil product is positioned as punishment, employment discipline or casual social conformity.
