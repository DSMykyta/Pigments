Access

Prescription, hospital, expanded and restricted pathways

Ordinate does not sell prescription medicines directly to the public. Access depends on local approval, clinician review, facility status and institutional authorization.

Prescription

Local prescriber pathway

For outpatient and specialist medicines available under country-specific labeling.

Hospital-only

Facility supply

For acute care medicines used in surgery, emergency care or specialist wards.

Expanded access

Managed patient request

For serious unmet need where local law permits physician-led access.

Restricted

Institutional programs

For military medical units, state pigment agencies, containment systems, emergency authorities and licensed research facilities.

Expanded access request principles

Requests require a licensed physician, documented diagnosis or use context, benefit-risk rationale, local regulatory allowance and safety reporting plan. Expanded access is not promotion and does not guarantee product supply.

Restricted programs

Operational coordinate medicines are never requested through patient access channels.
