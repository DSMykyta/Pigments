Pharmacovigilance

Safety reporting and communications

Ordinate monitors benefit-risk information before and after launch, including clinical study data, post-market reports, quality complaints, labeling updates and potential recalls.

Institutional incidents involving controlled coordinate medicines.

Labeling updates, risk minimization and Dear Patient notices.

Healthcare Professional Letters

HCP-only letters and medical safety updates.

How safety information is handled

Reports are reviewed for seriousness, expectedness, possible causal association, country requirements and product labeling impact. Pigment-specific reports may also include axis, activation type, visible glow, conscious control and injury to self, others or property.

Emergency note

This site is not an emergency service. Immediate danger, severe injury or uncontrolled activation should be handled through local emergency systems.
