Restricted institutional access

This page describes the access pathway for Ordinate medicines that are not supplied through public pharmacy. It is provided for transparency and is intended for the authorized institutional users named below. It is not a marketing communication and does not constitute an offer to supply.

Scope

Eligible institutional users

Restricted institutional access is available only to organizations operating under formal authorization in the country in which the medicine will be used. Eligibility is determined by Ordinate, in consultation with the relevant national authority, on a per-program basis.

  • Military medical units
  • State pigment regulation agencies
  • Prison and containment medical systems
  • Emergency response medical authorities
  • Licensed research facilities
  • Olympic and professional sport regulation bodies
  • Courts and forensic medicine services
Out of scope

Who cannot apply

Restricted institutional access is not available to private companies seeking to procure medicines for their own personnel outside an approved national program, to private security organizations, to non-licensed research entities, or to any party intending to use a restricted Ordinate medicine outside the scope of the relevant authorization.

Requests outside the scope above will not be processed and may be referred to the appropriate regulator.

Process

How an authorized institution applies

Confirm eligibility

The applying institution confirms that it operates under formal authorization in its jurisdiction and that the requested medicine falls within the scope of that authorization.

Designate an institutional contact

The institution designates a named medical or regulatory officer who will act as the responsible point of contact for all communications, supply, supervision and reporting.

Submit institutional inquiry

The designated contact submits an institutional inquiry through the channel below, including the institution's authorization reference.

Ordinate review

Ordinate medical affairs, regulatory affairs and the safety committee review the inquiry, in consultation with the relevant national authority where required.

Bilateral agreement

If the inquiry is approved, a bilateral institutional agreement is concluded covering supply, supervision, reporting, audit and termination.

Supervised supply and ongoing reporting

Supply is provided under the agreed supervision framework, with mandatory pharmacovigilance and protocol reporting throughout the program.

Contact

Institutional inquiry channel

Inquiries from authorized institutions are received via the institutional inquiry channel. The form requires the institution's authorization reference. Inquiries lacking valid authorization data are not processed.

Oversight

Government oversight and transparency

All restricted institutional programs are subject to ongoing oversight by the Ordinate Safety Committee and by the relevant national authority. Aggregate data on the volume and category of restricted programs is published annually in the Ordinate Transparency Report. Individual contracts and patient-level data are not published.
