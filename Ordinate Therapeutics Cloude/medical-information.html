Ordinate/ Medical Information

Medical information

Ordinate Medical Information answers medical and scientific questions from patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals and authorized institutions. We supply patient leaflets, prescribing information and educational materials in line with country regulation. Standard response time is five working days; urgent inquiries are handled within twenty-four hours.

Pediatric resources

Pediatric medical information

Ordinate Pediatric Medical Information supports clinicians, schools and family-medicine programs caring for pediatric and adolescent pigment carriers. Materials are reviewed annually and translated for the regions in which Axisol is approved.

  • Pediatric prescribing summary (HCP)
  • School integration guide for pediatric carriers
  • Family communication leaflet — talking with your child about pigment expression
  • Adolescent stabilization decision aid
