Pregnancy & pediatric exposure

Ordinate maintains a long-term exposure registry for patients who took an Ordinate medicine during pregnancy, who breastfed under exposure, or whose pediatric exposure (intentional or accidental) is documented. The registry feeds long-term follow-up — please report even if no event has occurred.
