Neurocalm

neuromodulating compound

Anxiolytic neuromodulator for chronic anxiety and stress states.

Prescription onlyPatient guide available

About Neurocalm

Neurocalm is a prescription medicine indicated for the treatment of chronic anxiety states, generalized anxiety disorder and stress-related somatic symptoms. It is also prescribed to patients in whom stress provokes physical reactions, including pigment carriers experiencing stress-induced involuntary activation.

Neurocalm is one of Ordinate's broadest-prescribed medicines and is supplied through the standard prescription channel in 47 countries.

Use contexts

  • Chronic anxiety and generalized anxiety disorder
  • Stress-induced somatic symptoms (palpitations, tremor, gastrointestinal)
  • Stress-driven involuntary pigment activation, in conjunction with appropriate pigment medicine
  • Adjustment-related anxiety after acute medical events

Healthcare professional information

Neurocalm is indicated for the treatment of chronic anxiety states. Initiate at low dose and titrate to clinical effect. Consider concurrent suppressor therapy in pigment-carrier patients with documented stress-triggered activation.

Mechanism profile

Neurocalm provides anxiolytic neuromodulation through central inhibitory pathway support without sedative bias. The pharmacological profile minimizes daytime impairment and shows favorable interaction with maintenance pigment suppressors. Detailed pharmacological data are provided in the prescribing information.

Safety information

Discontinue and contact a clinician if the patient experiences severe drowsiness, allergic symptoms, mood deterioration or new pigment activation patterns.

Adverse event reporting

Suspected adverse events should be reported through Ordinate's pharmacovigilance channel.
