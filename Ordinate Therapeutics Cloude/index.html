Three pillars

One company, three operating environments.

Ordinate is organized around the populations and protocols our medicines must serve — from public pharmacy to controlled institutional supply.

01 · General medicine

Public & specialist therapies

Anxiolytics, analgesics, cardiovascular and metabolic therapies for the broad civilian population, including pigment carriers and non-carriers alike.

02 · Pigment integration

Living with a pigment

Suppressors, blockers, recovery agents and ophthalmic therapies that allow pigment carriers to live, work and study without involuntary activation.

03 · Restricted programs

Institutional supply only

Coordinate-modifying and containment therapies developed for authorized military, forensic and emergency-response medical programs under strict government supervision.

Pigment medicine

One in ten people lives with a pigment.

Pigment medicine is one of three pillars of Ordinate, but it is the area where regulatory, ethical and clinical questions are most acute. Our therapies are designed to help carriers integrate into ordinary life — school, work, sport, public space — without surrendering autonomy.

Civilian therapies
9 approved medicines for daily integration, suppression, ophthalmic and dermatological care.
Pediatric programs
Adolescent stabilization (Axisol) and pediatric exposure registry support across 18 countries.
Sports medicine
Recovery and post-activation neural support (Kinetra, Synaptide-P) under sport-regulator approval.
Restricted
Coordinate-modifying medicines available only through authorized institutional programs.

Pharmacovigilance

Patient safety is monitored across the entire product lifecycle.

Pharmacovigilance at Ordinate covers clinical development, post-marketing data, our institutional safety database, labeling updates and — when necessary — recalls. Reports from patients, healthcare professionals, sport regulators and authorized institutions feed the same global system.

Adverse events involving pigment activation, breakthrough glow, unexpected blockade or restricted-product incidents must be reported through the appropriate channel.
