Ordinate/ Safety

Safety & pharmacovigilance

Patient safety is monitored across the entire product lifecycle — from clinical development to post-marketing surveillance. Reports from patients, healthcare professionals, sport regulators and authorized institutions feed Ordinate's global pharmacovigilance system. If you suspect an adverse event with an Ordinate product, you may report it through the form below or through your country's regulator.
