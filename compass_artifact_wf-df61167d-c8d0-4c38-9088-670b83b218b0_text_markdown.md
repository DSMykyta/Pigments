# The Pigments system, measured against the canon

The fictional "Pigments" magic system — HSV-coordinate abilities (Hue as domain, Saturation as focus, Value as power), exactly five "Special Grade" individuals globally at V<20 whose powers become reality-laws, RGB-averaging genetic inheritance with mutation maintenance, a private corporate classifier named "Pantone," and a UN-held "Black Cylinder" that renders every carrier as an anonymous colored dot — is a remarkably over-engineered worldbuilding construct whose structural choices can be defended, point by point, against a deep body of precedent. Across ten analytic axes — Sanderson's craft laws, shonen classification, mutant-gene fiction, color-light magic, bureaucratic magic, panopticon theory, real-world antitrust, rare-event epistemology, population genetics, and narrative-craft theory — **Pigments emerges as a compression of genre moves most fantasy systems make individually, combined in a single coherent apparatus that is unusually literate about its own trade-offs.** The most important finding is that its apparent novelty concentrates in three places: the orthogonal three-axis continuous coordinate space, the diegetic corporate-as-Mendeleev classifier, and the explicit mutation-rescued blending-inheritance model. On the remaining dimensions it is sophisticated, but precedented.

## Where the system sits on Sanderson's spectrum

Pigments is a textbook Sanderson hybrid. Brandon Sanderson's First Law (2007) — "an author's ability to solve conflict with magic is directly proportional to how well the reader understands said magic" — is satisfied almost automatically because **HSV is a real taxonomy readers already know from graphic software**. His Second Law ("Limitations > Powers," 2012) is over-satisfied: V-bounded rarity, HSV-coordinate closure, genetic averaging, and the corporate classifier are all active limitations, several of them *institutionally enforced in-world*, a move Sanderson never reaches for. His Third Law ("Expand what you already have," 2013) is honored maximally — a continuous 3D cylinder has no peer among Cosmere grids for extrapolation surface, and the Zeroth Law ("err on the side of what is awesome," from signings) is served by the exactly-five Special Grade cap.

The deepest Sanderson comparison is Allomancy's 16-metal 4×4 grid (physical/mental/temporal/enhancement × push/pull × internal/external) and Stormlight's Double Eye of ten Orders sharing ten Surges on a 10-cycle. Both are **discrete 2D lattices**; Sanderson never builds a full 3D continuous coordinate space, and critically **he never makes power magnitude an independent axis** — strength in the Cosmere is a function of Investiture supply, ideals sworn, or Breaths accumulated, never an orthogonal coordinate. The Warbreaker Heightenings (1st through 10th, with ~50 → 50,000 Breaths required) offer the closest rarity-scaling precedent and the 10th-Heightening / God-King tier is the nearest structural analog to Special Grade — but Heightenings are **acquired** via Breath transfer, not born into, so the comparison illuminates how unusual it is to make a bounded elite both genetic and reality-law-capable.

**Pigments' genuine novelty relative to the Cosmere** is threefold: scalar magnitude as a coordinate (V), a quantitative-genetic inheritance algorithm, and an in-world Mendeleev-role institution. The first two have no Sanderson analog; the third has only the authorial paratext of the Ars Arcanum appendices.

## The five Special Grades are genre-standard, not idiosyncratic

The "exactly five named top-tier threats" choice is the **modal count across post-2000 battle shonen**: Jujutsu Kaisen's Special Grade Curses (Sukuna, Mahito, Jogo, Hanami, Dagon), One Piece's Yonko (4), Hunter × Hunter's Chimera Ant Royal Guards (3), Fairy Tail's Ten Wizard Saints, Demon Slayer's Upper Moons (6). The canonical rationale, formalized across TV Tropes' *Quirky Miniboss Squad*, *Standard Evil Organization Squad*, and *Shitennō* pages: **a bounded oligarchy delivers a reachable ending, supports fan ranking discourse, and permits full individuation without cast bloat**. Five is large enough for internal faction drama, small enough that readers can memorize the roster and argue the rankings.

The genre's critical self-consciousness about this is visible in two related shifts. First, *Dragon Ball*'s scouter (1988) is the genre's **canonical failure mode** — a continuous numerical power measure that broke catastrophically during the Frieza saga. Later shonen replaced it with **discrete, power-law-skewed tiers**: HxH Nen rarity, JJK's Grade 4/3/2/1/Special pyramid, MHA Hero Rankings. Pigments' V<20 hard cap is the purest implementation of this post-scouter solution; because the coordinate is bounded, numerical escalation is structurally impossible.

The Nen hexagon of Hunter × Hunter — Enhancer / Emitter / Manipulator / Transmuter / Conjurer / Specialist, arranged as six points with adjacency affinities at 100%/80%/60%/40% — is **the single closest structural neighbor to Pigments' six hue domains**. Togashi's design choices map onto Pigments with disquieting fidelity: Water Divination is a Pantone diagnostic; Specialization is the outlier slot that Pigments' continuous HSV arguably eliminates. Pigments' advantage over Nen is dimensional: continuous 360° hue instead of six discrete spokes, plus two additional orthogonal axes (S, V).

Jujutsu Kaisen's Domain Expansion is the genre's clearest precedent for **a soft reality-law ceiling atop an otherwise hard system** — a "sure-hit, sure-kill" technique reserved to the Special Grade tier. Gojo's Limitless / Infinity (inherited clan technique plus the Six Eyes, first co-occurrence in 400 years) is the nearest match to Pigments' V<20 "Singularity" spatial compression. Both are bounded absolute techniques; Pigments arguably improves by making its spatial law **systemic** rather than a single clan's hereditary privilege.

## The color-magic lineage: Brent Weeks is the real test case

Brent Weeks' Lightbringer series (2010–2019) is the only major Western fantasy with an explicit color-indexed, institutionally-classified, physical-cost-bearing magic — and the comparison with Pigments is the most revealing in the whole corpus. Weeks' drafters channel a **1D spectrum** (sub-red through superviolet, plus outer-spectrum paryl and chi, plus mythical black and white); each color has a defined luxin material, personality, vice/virtue, and scent. The Chromeria gates classification and training. Drafters pay in their halo — residual iris color that expands until breaking, after which they go mad and become color wights. The Freeing (ritual suicide by Prism on Sun Day) is the institutional response.

**Pigments' key advance over Lightbringer is dimensional**. Where Weeks works with a spectrum + outer-spectrum list (with "disjunctive bichromes" requiring special pleading — Weeks admits "it is unknown how or why" they exist), HSV coordinates naturally accommodate any point in the cylinder. Pigments' saturation-as-focus and value-as-power axes have **no Lightbringer analog** — Weeks conflates these into spectral breadth (monochrome → bichrome → polychrome → Prism) plus willpower plus lifespan budget. But Lightbringer wins on **sensory tactility**: sticky red, crystalline blue, springy green are iconic in a way a point in a continuous cylinder struggles to be. Pigments needs a somatic signature as legible as the halo to match Weeks' storytelling hooks.

N.K. Jemisin's Broken Earth trilogy supplies the **state-violence template** Pigments will need if it wants moral weight: the Fulcrum's ring-ranking (1-ring novice to 10-ring senior, Alabaster as the unique 10-ringer), the Node Maintainers (orogene children lobotomized and wired into seismic infrastructure), and Guardians as state handlers. Jemisin deliberately *refuses* phenotypic markers for orogenes to prevent racial-coding; Pigments, by giving carriers a literal visible color, re-engages exactly the racialization Jemisin avoided. Whether this is a flaw or a deliberate critique depends on the author's intent, but it cannot go unremarked.

R.F. Kuang (Poppy War, Babel) supplies the **extractive-classification register** most closely matching Pantone's commercial framing: magic as a system that extracts value from classified bodies, the institution as complicit. Her 2022 PopMatters interview on Babel's silver-working — "translation can never be perfect" — models the critique Pigments could lean into: classification as a system whose imperfections are its business model.

## The mutant-gene family: where Pigments innovates most

The comparison to X-Men, Wild Cards, and Worm exposes Pigments' **most original structural move** — the combination of continuous genetic inheritance with a fixed-count elite tier.

Marvel's X-gene, as formalized by Hickman's *House of X* (2019), runs Epsilon / Beta / Alpha / Omega, but "Omega-level" is attached to a *specific power* ("an undefinable upper limit of that power's specific classification") with no hard global count. The category has been renegotiated three times since Claremont coined it in *Uncanny X-Men* #208 (1986), and comic critics at CBR and Screen Rant flag ongoing inconsistency. Inheritance is non-Mendelian and essentially hand-waved; Cyclops and Jean's children (Cable, Rachel, Nate Grey) have unrelated powersets. The 2024 *Frontiers in Genetics* paper by Grimsted et al. argues the "X-gene" framing "hollows out" difference because any heterogeneous human variation can be reduced to one mutated locus. Pigments' RGB-averaging with explicit mutation input is **strictly more rigorous** than anything Marvel puts on the page.

George R. R. Martin's Wild Cards is the closest statistical parallel: Xenovirus Takis-A, released 1946, hits with a 90% Black Queen / 9% Joker / 1% Ace distribution. Martin and physicist Ian Tregillis's 2024 paper "Ergodic Lagrangian Dynamics in a Superhero Universe" formally models this as a state-vector dynamical system. Pigments **refines rather than replaces** the Wild Cards frame: three discrete buckets become a continuous HSV manifold, and the 1% Ace tier becomes a power-law-rarefied 5-person Special Grade cap.

Worm (Wildbow, 2011–13) is **the fiction with the most granular classifier** — twelve PRT tags (Brute/Mover/Shaker/Striker/Thinker/Stranger/Master/Blaster/Tinker/Trump/Breaker/Changer) plus threat numbers 1–12, yielding combined labels like "Shaker 5 / Brute 7." But Worm's categories are notoriously tactical-not-analytical (fan-critic vigaroe.com documents extensive inconsistency), and the threat number is **uncapped** (Endbringers approach infinity). Pigments' HSV avoids Worm's taxonomic overlap by generating every ability from a unique coordinate, and its bounded top tier avoids the Endbringer escalation problem.

On inheritance specifically: My Hero Academia's Quirks use a **three-way lottery** (one parent's, the other's, or a fusion — Todoroki's Half-Hot-Half-Cold is not a lukewarm blend), with "Quirk Marriage" as an explicit eugenic practice the narrative condemns (Endeavor's abuse of Rei) and Garaki's Quirk Singularity Theory as the runaway failure mode. Naruto's Kekkei Genkai are discrete categorical combinations (Ice = Water + Wind, Wood = Water + Earth). **Only Pigments uses averaging**, which is structurally identical to the 19th-century blending inheritance model that real biology abandoned because Fleeming Jenkin's 1867 critique of Darwin showed it would halve variation each generation and swamp adaptation. Bulmer's 2004 *British Journal for the History of Science* paper argues Jenkin's swamping argument can be rescued **exactly by sufficiently high mutation rates** — which is what Pigments explicitly does. The system is therefore a worked example of the Jenkin problem plus its mutational solution, making it the **only major genre magic system directly engaging the history of quantitative genetics**. Fisher's 1918 infinitesimal model and Lande's 1975–76 quantitative-genetics papers supply the mathematical machinery that would complete the formalization.

The sobering side of this rigor: because averaging makes two bright parents reliably produce a bright child (unlike particulate recessive traits where carriers hide), Pigments' inheritance rule **strengthens the logical case for Lebensborn-style breeding programs** within the fiction. This is Galton 1886 → Buck v. Bell 1927 → Kevles 1985 territory, and a responsible treatment must confront it head-on.

## Pantone's antitrust exposure is not hypothetical

The fictional Pantone's situation — owning the classification standard, selling spectrographs that measure against it, running PMS Connect SaaS, consulting on outputs, facing EU pressure — maps with precision onto **live 21st-century regulatory fact patterns**.

The most direct parallel is the **Adobe-Pantone dispute of 2022–2023**: real Pantone withdrew color libraries from Adobe Creative Cloud, required a separate Pantone Connect subscription (~$15/month), and caused legacy design files using delisted swatches to render spot-color channels as **solid black with a message that licensing had changed**. Ars Technica, NPR, and TidBITS covered the backlash; artist Stuart Semple's free "Freetone" library collected ~30,000 downloads. The image of a designer's archive turning black because a classification subscription lapsed is functionally the same image as a hospital losing patient-classification access when PMS Connect billing fails.

The **European Commission's Google AdTech case** (Statement of Objections June 2023; €2.95B fine September 2025 with Commissioner Ribera ordering a conflict-of-interest remedy within 60 days) is the **single most on-point antitrust precedent** for the fictional scenario. The Commission's exact language — "inherent conflict of interest," "standard and products in the same hands," structural divestiture as the only adequate remedy — is the language a fictional regulator would use against Pantone. Google Shopping (2017, €2.42B, self-preferencing doctrine affirmed at the Court of Justice in 2024), Google Android (2018, €4.34B, tying), and Google AdSense for Search (2019, €1.49B, exclusivity) collectively supply the toolkit. Microsoft's 1998–2001 US case and 2004 EU case (interoperability-disclosure remedy) establish tying doctrine and the API-publication remedy that would be Pantone's likely forced remedy.

Other useful parallels: **ARM Holdings** (pure IP-licensor whose Qualcomm litigation ended October 2025 with Arm losing, illustrating how standards-owners can be coerced by downstream licensees); **Dolby** (certification-stamp gatekeeping under royalty-plus-audit); **SWIFT** (Belgian cooperative whose 2022 Russian-bank disconnection showed how private standards become geopolitical weapons under EU Regulation 2022/345); **Visa/Mastercard** (DOJ September 2024 suit against Visa for debit-network monopolization); and the foundational **Standard Oil breakup (1911)** establishing structural divestiture for vertically-integrated monopolies.

Two cases specifically underwrite the risk of **a classification monopolist also holding subject data**: **23andMe's October 2023 breach** (6.9M users scraped, March 2025 Chapter 11 bankruptcy, genetic database sold to TTAM, $30M settlement finalized January 2026) shows the transfer risk on classifier insolvency; **Equifax 2017** ($575M FTC/CFPB/state settlement) and **FICO's 2025 partial unseating by VantageScore at Fannie/Freddie** show both the breach risk and the policy trajectory toward forced alternatives. All of this strongly suggests the fictional EU case against Pantone is not a speculation but a plausible 2020s regulatory narrative.

## Max Gladstone is the bureaucratic-magic literary anchor

Among the bureaucratic magic traditions, **Max Gladstone's Craft Sequence** (2012–present; *Three Parts Dead*, *Four Roads Cross*, *Wicked Problems* 2024) is the deepest literary treatment of magic-as-institution and the best template for Pantone's satirical register. Gladstone has explicitly named James C. Scott's *Seeing Like a State* (1998) as the series' conceptual keystone, and his interviews describe Craft as "legal thrillers about faith, or religious thrillers about law and finance" with gods as "metaphors for corporations" and magicians as "lawyers drawing power from contracts, rare knowledge, and dead languages." Gladstone does not have a monopoly classifier on Pantone's scale (necromantic firms compete), but his treatment of soulstuff-as-currency, debt-zombie labor, and hostile creditor takeovers of gods is the genre's sharpest political register.

**Robert Jackson Bennett's Founders trilogy** (*Foundryside* 2018, *Shorefall* 2020, *Locklands* 2022) is the next closest: scriving as literal proprietary code owned by four merchant houses, a central "lexicon" that works like a magical server (scrived objects fail when disconnected — the SaaS analog is precise), and explicit cyberpunk-as-fantasy framing ("I explicitly came into this work wanting it to be cyberpunk dressed up as fantasy," Bennett told *Les Chroniques du Chroniqueur*, 2022). Pantone's platform business model has **no closer literary precedent**.

Harry Potter's Ministry of Magic provides the dystopian-bureaucracy register (MuggleNet 2004, *New Statesman* 2017, Ilias Yocaris in *Le Monde diplomatique* 2003, Bethany Barratt's *The Politics of Harry Potter* 2012, Patrick O'Malley's *Bureaucracy and Power in Fiction* 2017). Charles Stross's Laundry Files is the **inverse control**: what happens when classification is state-run and top-secret rather than private and public. Lev Grossman's Brakebills Disciplines are the credentialing parallel without the commercial layer. Genevieve Cogman's Invisible Library is the meta-institutional parallel. Susanna Clarke's Norrell is the one-man aspirational monopolist.

**Pantone's genuine differentiation within this lineage**: the "discovered, not invented" Mendeleev framing has **no literary precedent** in the surveyed corpus. Gladstone's Craft is explicitly invented; Bennett's scriving is human engineering; Stross's magic is applied mathematics Turing formalized; Rowling's categories are ethnographic-feeling but never scientifically grounded. Framing Pantone as a discoverer of natural law — rather than an inventor of conventions — is the critical move that generates the characteristic ambivalence of the fictional scenario: **the claim of neutrality is stronger and the conflict of interest is therefore sharper**.

## The Black Cylinder is an inverted panopticon and a dread-object

The Cylinder is structurally **two things at once**, and neither is straightforward surveillance. Geometrically it is a Bentham panopticon — circular, with every magically-classified human reduced to a dot on the periphery or volume. But it inverts every axis of the classical surveillance paradigm: classical surveillance identifies individuals and hides the observer; the Cylinder **anonymizes absolutely** and sits transparently at the UN. It refuses Foucault's (*Discipline and Punish*, 1975) "automatic functioning of power" through internalized gaze — subjects cannot internalize being watched when watching cannot single them out. Thomas Mathiesen's 1997 synopticon ("the many watch the few") is closer but still fails: the Cylinder shows the few as *anonymous statistical mass*, not as celebrities.

The Cylinder is therefore best read as **an analog differential-privacy device** (Dwork 2006): each dot is k-anonymous in its color bin; a single subject cannot be re-identified from the aggregate — **unless they become the aggregate**, which is exactly the designed tripwire for a #000000 carrier. Helen Nissenbaum's contextual integrity (2004) flags the normative question: the Cylinder creates a new context in which existence-as-power-category is a universally-transmitted datum, and the question of whether this is normatively appropriate has no prior norm to import. Roger Clarke's 1988 "dataveillance" framework is technically satisfied — mass-dataveillance without the identification stage — which solves Clarke's hard problem by refusing it.

The tripwire register is equally important. The Cylinder is a **dread-object** in the tradition of the Doomsday Clock (85 seconds to midnight as of 27 January 2026, the closest ever), the DEW Line / NORAD, and Soviet Perimeter / "Dead Hand." Its daily informational content is near-zero; its value lies almost entirely in the phase transition it would undergo if a #000000 subject appeared. **This is the epistemology of fail-deadly alarms**: instruments whose purpose is to detect events that may never occur, justified solely by the catastrophic cost of missing them. Giorgio Agamben's *Homo Sacer* (1995) frame completes the reading: the five Special Grade individuals (and especially a hypothetical #000000) are **extra-juridical** — included in the polity by exclusion from its ordinary categories, visible as bare life stripped of political form. A #000000 carrier would be the limit figure of homo sacer: bare life so intense it would obliterate the representational apparatus itself, turning the Cylinder black.

Deleuze's "Postscript on the Societies of Control" (1990) provides the deepest fit — his description of "a control mechanism, giving the position of any element within an open environment at any given instant" reads as a specification document for the Cylinder, but the Cylinder **pushes past Deleuze's endpoint**: the "dividual" (a person fragmented into data) is reduced not to a dossier of fragments but to a single coordinate triple in a phase space. All ordinary dividual traits collapse into one color code.

## The data hole as Kuhnian anomaly

The institutional predicament — reliable models for common carriers, extrapolated models for intermediates, "terra incognita" at the N=5 tail with Special Grade abilities listed as "predicted" not "confirmed" — is simultaneously multiple recognizable epistemological structures. It is a textbook **Extreme Value Theory** scenario (Fisher–Tippett–Gnedenko 1928/1943; Embrechts, Klüppelberg, Mikosch 1997) where Gumbel / Fréchet / Weibull parameter uncertainty dominates. It is pure **Extremistan** in Nassim Taleb's sense (*The Black Swan*, 2007): "the supreme law of Mediocristan" — no single observation dominates — breaks down, and Taleb's "tyranny of the singular" applies. It is a **rare-disease regulatory structure** (Orphan Drug Act 1983; EU Regulation EC 141/2000; NIH Undiagnosed Diseases Network since 2013) in which statistical inference gives way to deep phenotyping and N-of-1 trials.

It is especially **Kuhnian**: Thomas Kuhn's *Structure of Scientific Revolutions* (1962) describes exactly the dynamic where a paradigm's extrapolation predicts phenomena it cannot actually characterize, and the stubbornness of the anomaly is proportional to its revolutionary potential. The "predicted but not confirmed" label may itself be a paradigm-preserving move — an institutional gesture that treats the N=5 as soluble puzzles rather than paradigm-breakers. Ian Hacking's "Making Up People" (*LRB*, 2006) completes the picture with the **looping effect**: even the provisional classification already does work on the five, creating expectation structures and possibly self-concept changes before empirical confirmation.

Bowker and Star's *Sorting Things Out* (MIT Press, 1999) names what the institutions are doing to the five: forcing them into a **residual category** — the ICD's NEC, DSM's NOS — and producing **torque**, "the twisting that occurs when a formal classification system is mismatched with an individual's biographical trajectory." The RAND-developed **Decision-Making Under Deep Uncertainty** framework (Lempert, Popper & Bankes 2003) prescribes the policy response: stop trying to predict what the five are like and instead stress-test institutional responses across a deliberately broad envelope of what they could be, seeking robustness rather than optimality.

## What Pigments uniquely contributes

Across ten axes the claim that best survives scrutiny is this: **Pigments is not more imaginative than any individual precedent in any individual domain, but it is unusually disciplined about combining them into a single coherent apparatus that anticipates its own critiques.** Its three strongest original moves are:

- **Orthogonal three-axis continuous coordinate space.** No comparator — Sanderson Cosmere, Lightbringer, Nen, Allomancy — has a full continuous 3D ability-coordinate system with power magnitude as an independent axis. This is the cleanest dimensional advance.
- **RGB-averaging inheritance with explicit mutation maintenance.** This is the only genre magic system directly engaging the Jenkin–Darwin blending-inheritance problem and its mutational rescue (Bulmer 2004). It is structurally more rigorous than X-Men's hand-waved X-gene, MHA's three-way Quirk lottery, or Naruto's clan-endogamous Kekkei Genkai, and roughly comparable in rigor to Dune's Bene Gesserit program but addressing population-level variance preservation rather than individual-level genotype assembly.
- **Diegetic corporate-as-Mendeleev classifier with live antitrust exposure.** The "discovered not invented" framing is absent from the bureaucratic-magic literary canon (Gladstone, Bennett, Stross, Grossman, Rowling all have invented systems), and the SaaS / platform business model + EU antitrust layer has only Bennett's lexicon-as-magical-server as a literary precedent. The 2022–23 Adobe-Pantone dispute and the 2023–25 Google AdTech case supply the real regulatory template.

The weakest originality claims lie in its most imitable features: the exactly-five Special Grade cap is a genre-standard shonen move; the soft reality-law ceiling above a hard magic base is the Sanderson-hybrid / JJK Domain Expansion / HxH Specialist pattern; the state or corporate classification of genetic ability-carriers is X-Men, Wild Cards, Worm, and Broken Earth territory. These are successful rather than novel.

**The deepest critical risk** is one the system takes on by design: making pigment a literally visible color on people re-engages precisely the racialization N. K. Jemisin deliberately refused in orogeny, and RGB-averaging inheritance strengthens rather than weakens the logic of eugenic matching — pushing the fiction into the ethical terrain of Buck v. Bell and Lebensborn whether or not the author intends it. The Black Cylinder's anonymization protects against Foucauldian individuation but not against Deleuzian reduction to coordinate, and a #000000 tripwire is structurally Agambenian homo sacer. These are not necessarily flaws; they are the politics the design commits to, and the strongest reading of Pigments treats them as the system's central subject rather than its unintended residue.
