# BLAC PINC Lore Bible

Working file for the book/worldbuilding layer. The public site should read like a real K-pop artist site; this file explains what is actually happening in the Pantone world.

## Public Premise

BLAC PINC is a CHROMA HOUSE girl group modeled on a real Blackpink-style public presence: luxury fashion, global tours, high-contrast black/pink visuals, huge fandom, and official messaging that keeps conflict polished.

Current public lineup:

- JISUE: leader, main vocal, Soft Pink.
- HWAZA: new member, main rapper / lead dancer, Warm Rose.
- ROZAY: lead vocal / visual, Pale Pink.
- LIZAH: lead dance / maknae, Hot Pink.

Archive member:

- JENIE: former main rapper, Crown Pink, officially left for a solo career and OTM label work.

## Real Situation

JENIE was not simply "ready for solo work." During the Crown of Rose / Pink Venom era her pigment became unstable. The public scandal was framed as behavior, contract tension, and brand pressure, but PMS Bureau classified the real issue as an uncontrolled Tier 3 escalation.

Her ability makes language acquisition unnaturally easy, but specifically through Korean cultural and linguistic structures. That is politically uncomfortable in Korea: it looks like national soft power, idol training, education, and memory manipulation are all overlapping. The company cannot say this out loud, so the scandal story is useful cover.

JISUE becomes leader after JENIE's removal. This is emotionally true, not only corporate messaging: she is the stabilizer of the group and the only one CHROMA HOUSE trusts to keep the public formation calm.

HWAZA is recruited because her pigment profile can replace the performance function JENIE left behind. She is not a perfect one-to-one replacement. She is warmer, rougher, more direct, and more dangerous in live rap sections because her ability travels through voice and rhythm.

## Member Powers

JISUE / Soft Pink:

- Ability: empathic calm.
- Trigger: direct camera or eye contact.
- Public translation: "leader aura", trustworthy vocals, ballad center.
- Cost: exhaustion and delayed emotional rebound.

HWAZA / Warm Rose:

- Ability: sonic persuasion.
- Trigger: rap cadence, repeated hooks, speech at performance BPM.
- Public translation: "charisma", "stage control", "she owns the room."
- Cost: all rap lyrics are reviewed by PMS before release.

ROZAY / Pale Pink:

- Ability: glamour aura.
- Trigger: passive presence in frame, especially fashion/editorial settings.
- Public translation: luxury ambassador magnetism.
- Cost: brands and people around her become hard to separate from her image.

LIZAH / Hot Pink:

- Ability: combat ecstasy / imitative movement response.
- Trigger: high-speed choreography and airborne pigment dust.
- Public translation: dance virality, crowd energy, challenge culture.
- Cost: closed venues need ventilation and crowd monitoring.

JENIE / Crown Pink:

- Ability: accelerated language learning and linguistic mimicry, strongest through Korean.
- Trigger: listening, repetition, code-switching, lyric memorization.
- Public translation: multilingual interviews, rap diction, global celebrity fluency.
- Cost: Korea treats her as culturally sensitive; PMS treats her as politically unstable.
