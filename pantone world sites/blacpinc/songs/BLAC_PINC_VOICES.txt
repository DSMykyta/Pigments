Vocal: husky alto rap, sassy confident flow, Jennie-style cool detachment with subtle vocal melody. Low-to-mid range with slight nasal quality. Delivery — relaxed swagger, never straining, word-play focused.

Прототип: Jennie Kim (BLACKPINK).

Прототип: Jisoo (BLACKPINK).

Найкраще у: ballad-leads, cinematic intros, chorus anchor vocals, controlled high notes. Уникати: fast rap, raspy growls.
