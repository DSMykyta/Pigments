BIOGRAPHY 経歴

Born in Yokohama to a steel-industry family, Saito's pigment awakened at age 13 during a middle-school basketball tournament — a red flash from his shooting hand that set the opposing team's ball rebound into a flaming spiral. Parents brought him to the Tokyo PMS Institute within 72 hours for certification, per post-2019 protocol.

Three seasons with Osaka Flame U-20 (2020–2022), then drafted #2 overall by Tokyo Oni in 2023. Averaged 17.4 PPG as a rookie; promoted to Strike starter midway through the 2024 championship run. His Firebolt shot is now the most telegraphed — and still most effective — single-player action in T1.

"Shooting without the glow is a lie. Let everyone see it coming. Let them miss anyway." — SAITO · POST-MATCH · OSAKA 2026.02.08
