Safety & pharmacovigilance

Patient safety is monitored across the entire product lifecycle — from clinical development to post-marketing surveillance. Reports from patients, healthcare professionals, sport regulators and authorized institutions feed Ordinate's global pharmacovigilance system. Use the appropriate channel below for your situation.

Report channels

What kind of report do you need to submit?

Each channel feeds the same global pharmacovigilance database, but the form fields and priority differ.

Adverse event

Report an adverse event

Any unwanted medical event in a patient using an Ordinate medicine, including pigment-specific events.

 Quality

Product quality complaint

Suspected defect, contamination, packaging or label issue.

 Error

Medication error

Wrong dose, wrong product or administration error.

 Registry

Pregnancy / pediatric exposure

Exposure during pregnancy or in a minor, even without observed event.

 Pigment

Pigment activation incident

Involuntary activation, breakthrough glow, unexpected blockade or loss of conscious access.

 Restricted

Restricted product incident

Incident involving an Ordinate restricted, military or institutional medicine.
