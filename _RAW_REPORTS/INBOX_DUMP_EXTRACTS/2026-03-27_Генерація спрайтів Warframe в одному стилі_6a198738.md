## Summary

**Conversation Overview**

The person (Микита) is developing a visual novel game featuring 63 original characters called "farframes" — characters wearing Warframe-inspired protoframe suits. All character sprites are AI-generated (not official DE assets). The core goal throughout the conversation was establishing a reliable, repeatable pipeline to create character sprites in a consistent art style matching an existing Arthur character sprite: semi-realistic digital painting with warm muted skin tones, painterly brushstroke texture, neutral grey background, and T-pose mannequin body format.

The conversation progressed through extensive trial and error across multiple tools. ComfyUI (cloud.comfy.org) was explored for style transfer (VAEStyleTransferLatent), pose control (Pose to Image, DWPose, ControlNet/OpenPose), and expression editing (ExpressionEditor/AdvancedLivePortrait), but most workflows failed due to incompatible VAE models, platform limitations, and missing blueprints. The working pipeline that emerged uses Gemini for all generation tasks: a chat is initialized with multiple Arthur reference sprites to lock in style, then base mannequins (male muscular, female athletic) are generated once, followed by face swaps using cropped face references, skin tone matching, removal of cybernetic details, face closeup portraits, pose changes (arms/hands only, body forward-facing), and outfit application. Key prompts were refined iteratively — notably adding "REPAINT the face in the same art style" for face swaps, "NOT 3D render" negatives for texture, and "body facing directly forward, only copy arm positions" for poses. The person also identified that a female mannequin generated outside Arthur's chat context produced a visually inconsistent style, and that starting a single Gemini chat with Arthur references as the first message is critical for style consistency. For emotions (28 types catalogued for VN use), Gemini with descriptive expression prompts was settled on after ExpressionEditor failed on the platform. The person was frequently frustrated by circular advice and ComfyUI complexity, and explicitly corrected Claude multiple times for misattributing their own prompts and for suggesting unnecessary technical detours.
