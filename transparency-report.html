Scientific Ethics Council opinion

"In 2025 the Scientific Ethics Council reviewed seventeen pigment medicine programs. Fourteen received unconditional approval. Two received conditional approval subject to additional pediatric safeguards. One — the CRF-12 civilian Chromafinil derivative — was recommended for discontinuation on the grounds of unacceptable misuse potential. The Safety Committee accepted that recommendation. We commend the company for accepting the discontinuation despite its commercial implications, and for appointing an independent Pediatric Ethics Observer in early 2025."

— Scientific Ethics Council, annual opinion 2025-10-14
