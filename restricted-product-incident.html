Restricted product incident

This channel is for incidents involving Saturine, Chromafinil, Pallidex, Calibrant-6, Nullcord, Redline-C and other restricted institutional medicines. Reports must come from authorized institutional users. Public submissions are not accepted through this channel.

Authorized institutional users only.

Restricted product incidents must be reported by the institution's designated medical or regulatory officer through the institutional channel. Use of this channel by non-authorized parties is not consistent with applicable law and may be referred to the relevant regulator.

Bilateral institutional contract reference required for processing.

Public observers

If a member of the public has witnessed an incident that they believe involved a restricted medicine, the appropriate channel is the relevant national regulator or, where life is at risk, local emergency services. Members of the public should not file restricted incidents through this site.
